## 6.4.1 — Governing idea

The portfolio should feel:

> **Human, intelligent, warm, grounded, contemporary and lived-in.**

Not:

> corporate, glossy, sterile, hyper-minimal, “tech startup”, influencer-polished, or stock-photo-perfect.

Photography introduces **texture and life**. Consistency comes from the interface system (type, spacing, lavender restraint, surfaces, scale, crop, motion) — not from making every photo look the same.

---

## 6.4.2 — Six visual families

### FAMILY 01 — HUMAN

**Purpose:** presence without corporate performance.

- Prefer naturally observed Black/African people  
- Register: thoughtful, relaxed, capable, curious, warm, engaged  
- **Avoid:** arms-crossed executive, handshake, forced smile, pointing at charts, staged laptop meeting, “African entrepreneur” cliché, boardroom, exaggerated success  
- Lighting: daylight / window / soft directional  
- Colour: natural, warm, slightly muted — no heavy teal/orange grade  
- Composition: off-centre OK; negative space valuable  

### FAMILY 02 — PLACE

**Purpose:** physical world without requiring faces.

- Architecture, streets, rooms, windows, desks-in-context, neighbourhood, urban transitions  
- Feeling: **lived-in rather than spectacular**  
- **Avoid:** generic skyscraper, Silicon Valley office, staged coworking, luxury office, tourist / “future Africa” cliché  
- Composition: geometry, frames, thresholds, shadows, negative space  

### FAMILY 03 — WORK

**Purpose:** credibility.

> **Real work beats photography.**

- Primary: real interfaces, sites, artefacts, screenshots  
- Existing evidence remains the foundation  
- Present as **designed artefacts**: screenshot → framing → context → explanation  
- **Avoid:** fake dashboards, fabricated metrics, generic SaaS mockups, stock laptop screens, invented UI  

### FAMILY 04 — DETAIL

**Purpose:** visual punctuation.

- Close, quiet, tactile: hands, paper, pen, book edge, keyboard, material, shadow, texture  
- Feels **discovered**, not staged productivity stock  

### FAMILY 05 — ABSTRACT / EDITORIAL

**Purpose:** ideas without an obvious photograph (Transformation, Thinking, sensitive Faith moments).

- Light, shadow, reflection, fabric, paper, glass, organic form, quiet colour fields  
- Lavender `#B79CED` may appear **indirectly** only — never force brand colour into photos  

### FAMILY 06 — PERSONAL

**Purpose:** Ola herself.

| Asset | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Home portrait | Immediate / recognisable / identity-led |
| About portrait | Editorial / grounded / contextual |

More visual authority than any acquired editorial image.

**Hard rule:** never manufacture additional “personal” photos to fill gaps. If no authentic personal image exists → use another family or nothing.

## 6.4.3 — Cross-family cohesion

1. Natural light (default)  
2. Real texture  
3. Controlled saturation  
4. Editorial framing  
5. Negative space  
6. Human scale  
7. Welcome imperfection  
8. Restraint — no visual shouting

## 6.4.4 — Asset-specific briefs

### A. Home — Who Is Ola · P1 · Detail / Place / Editorial

- Quiet tactile working environment  
- Notebook / paper / pen / keyboard / hands / daylight  
- Feeling: **thinking + making + working** — not “busy professional”  
- Landscape ~**3:2**, asymmetric, meaningful focal area  
- Related to About journey but **not identical**  

### B. About — Journey · P1 · Detail / Place

- Home = **working** · About = **reflecting**  
- More paper / books / writing / environment; less technology emphasis  
- Landscape ~**3:2**, contemplative  

### C. Homeschool Core — Learning · P1 · Detail / Human

- Real learning materials in use  
- Curiosity, warmth, concentration  
- **Avoid:** perfect classroom, kids staring at camera, rainbow “homeschool ad”  
- If not genuinely Ola’s environment → clearly editorial, not personal documentation  
- Landscape ~**3:2**  

### D. Transformation · P2 · Abstract / Editorial

- Prefer **no obvious person**  
- Concept: **movement through perspective** (threshold, light, reflection, layered materials)  
- Possibility + movement + maturity — not motivational-poster energy  

### E. Faith & Community · P2 · Human / Place

- Genuine-feeling community / service moment  
- **Must not** be presented as Ola’s church, TNCOD, or events  
- **Avoid:** worship hands, church stage, preacher mic, dramatic Bible, praying silhouette, “African church” stereotype  
- Service and community, not religious iconography for its own sake  

### F. Thinking · P2 · Abstract / Editorial / Detail

- No global photography requirement  
- Essay-specific vocabulary; optional image only when it earns place  
- Not a thumbnail grid

## 6.4.5 — Explicit prohibitions

- Generic corporate stock  
- Generic “African professional” stock  
- Fake client photography  
- AI images presented as real events  
- AI images presented as Ola  
- Fabricated VIIX work / fake screenshots  
- Generic laptop-on-desk filler  
- Excessive smiling/teamwork imagery  
- Reusing the same photograph across multiple pages  
- One photography treatment forced across every dimension  
- Giant full-width images merely because a section is text-heavy  
- Brand-colour manipulation that makes photos feel artificial  

---

## 6.4.6 — Responsive art direction (pre-implementation)

An image is not art-directed until Desktop → Tablet → Mobile behaviour is known.

- **Desktop:** composition + negative space  
- **Tablet:** protect focal point  
- **Mobile:** crop for meaning, not completeness  

**Portraits:**

- Home: figure clarity + immediate recognition  
- About: environmental context + editorial character  

---

## 6.4.7 — Acquisition hierarchy (for Phase 6.5)

1. Real user assets (if authentic and appropriate)  
2. Real project evidence  
3. High-quality editorial photography  
4. Purpose-created editorial art  
5. AI generation **only** for conceptual/editorial images that cannot reasonably be mistaken for documentation of a real person, client, event, or environment  

> AI is not automatically bad. **Misrepresentation is bad.**

## 6.4.9 — Final visual philosophy

Not: “Look at all these beautiful images.”

Instead:

> **“There is a real person here, doing real work, thinking about real things, in a real world.”**

- Home portrait → person  
- About portrait → depth  
- Work evidence → credibility  
- Editorial → atmosphere  
- Abstract → conceptual freedom  
- Empty space → breath
