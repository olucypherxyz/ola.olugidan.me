## 4. About — draft

**Job:** Meet her properly. Replace the leftover web-developer story.  
**Progression:** who I am now → how I got here → what I can actually do → how I think → what I am building → where I am going → who I am.

### Opening — who I am now

I lead technology and business work. I am CTO and Managing Director at VIIX Solutions.

Around that, the work has kept widening. I coach people who want to grow in their careers. I founded Homeschool Core. I care about education in a broader sense than school timetables. I am a wife and a homeschooling mother. Faith and showing up for people matter to me.

I do not experience those as separate brands. They are the same person, paying attention to different problems.

### How I got here

I did not start as a CTO.

I began in digital marketing. Then IT support — keeping systems running, helping people get their work done. I did CompTIA A+ because I needed a firmer technical floor. Web development came next: not as a title I was collecting, but because building was the way to solve what was in front of me.

Client work taught me something support work had already started: people, expectations, delivery. Leadership grew out of that. SEO was part of making the work visible and usable, not a side hobby. Technology leadership and then business leadership followed at VIIX. Coaching — helping people close the gap between where they are and where they need to be — is the latest widening of the same pattern.

Each stage added a kind of capacity. Support taught me systems under pressure. Building taught me how to make something real. Clients taught me judgement. Leadership taught me that the work is bigger than what I can personally type. Coaching is teaching me how to help someone else see their own gaps without pretending I have a finished method for everything.

I am still in that last part. I am comfortable saying so.

### What I can actually do

I can build. I can lead. I can teach. I can sit with something messy, research it, and give it a shape people can use.

I move between the plan and the doing. I do not need a problem to arrive already labelled. I would rather understand it, then choose the tool.

That is not a stack. It is how I have had to work.

### How I think

I tend to start with the problem before the technology.

I care about the root of a thing, not only the surface. I like taking something unclear, fragmented or difficult and giving it structure. I learn a lot by doing — I do not expect to know everything before I begin. I test, I refine, I would rather have substance than something that only looks finished.

Growth, for me, is becoming more capable, more intentional, and more useful. Not collecting a more impressive title.

### What I am building

VIIX is the main place my technology and business leadership lives.

I am also building a coaching practice for professionals who want to grow — early, real, not dressed up as a large programme.

Homeschool Core came out of my own start as a homeschool parent. That story has its own page.

There are other ideas I am still turning over. I am not going to list things that are not ready to be named.

### Where I am going

I want more capacity, more wisdom, and more useful impact. I do not have a single destination mapped. I am still evolving, and I expect the work to keep changing as I do.

### Who I am

I am a wife and a mother. Homeschooling has changed how I think about learning, responsibility, and what it means to prepare someone for life — not only for exams.

Faith matters to me. So does service: using what I know to help, staying in community, keeping learning. I do not use this site as a church noticeboard.

My family is private. Motherhood is part of the story. The details of my home are not.

If you want the work in more concrete terms, that is on Work. If you want to talk, that is Connect.

### Opening

This is for people who are already in professional life and can feel the gap between where they are and where they need to be.

I work with corporate professionals who feel stuck, or ready to grow and not sure how. People who keep missing promotions. People who can see that their digital tools — including AI — are behind the role they want. People who have been in situations at work and known, afterwards, that they could have handled them better.

I am not interested in a slogan about unlocking potential. I am interested in the actual gap.

Growth at work is often not a mystery. Something is missing: skill, judgement, fluency with tools, confidence in the room, a clearer picture of what the next level requires.

The movement I care about is simple. Where you are. What is in the way. What you need to build. Then becoming more able, and more ready, for what is next.

That can include digital tools, practical AI, how you handle work, how you make decisions, and the quieter professional skills that do not show up on a course outline.

### How this exists today

**Developing**

I call the work the Executive Digital Transformation and Career Acceleration Programme. The name is a working name. The thing itself is early.

There is a first one-to-one coaching engagement underway. I am learning from that, and using it to shape the broader programme.

1. Bespoke one-to-one coaching

Those are directions, not a shop window. I have not finalised pricing. I am not going to invent packages or results. I would rather you know that something real is happening, and that the larger version is still being built.

## 7. Homeschool Core — draft

**Job:** Founder story. Exists vs becoming. Framework at developing maturity. Philosophy in the story, not a quote tile.

### Origin

I started homeschooling in Nigeria in January 2023.

I did not arrive with a tidy plan. There was a lot of uncertainty. I was trying to work out what to do, and I kept wishing there was somewhere practical to stand — other parents, clearer guidance, resources that did not assume I already knew the language of homeschooling.

I started Homeschool Core to help other parents like myself demystify homeschooling, build a community, and create resources I wish I had as a mom starting homeschooling in Nigeria in 2023.

### What I believe about school and life

I do not think schooling is only what happens inside the four corners of a school. It is everything that contributes to a child becoming a well-rounded adult. That belief is why this work exists. It is not a caption I added afterwards.

### What it is now

**Established / developing**

Today Homeschool Core is guidance for people who are new to this, resources for parents, and a platform for that work. I already share concepts and ideas through [@homeschool.core](https://www.instagram.com/homeschool.core/).

### What it is becoming

**Future / in development — do not describe as launched**

I am building toward a stronger homeschool community, more educational ideas, workshops, frameworks, and programmes. Those are the direction. They are not all finished rooms you can walk into today.

### Confident Homeschool and the 6P Path

**Developing**

Confident Homeschool is a programme in development. The Confident Homeschool Blueprint sits inside that programme. The 6P Path is the framework I am developing around it — not a polished education product I am pretending has been running for years.

The six Ps, as they stand (her wording):

1. **Purpose** — Build your homeschooling vision.
2. **Profile** — Understand and personalize your child's learning journey.
3. **Plan** — Design a simple, strategic homeschool plan that works.
4. **Place** — Create a calm and focused learning environment at home.
5. **Pace** — Create a sustainable homeschool rhythm that honors your energy and lifestyle.
6. **Partnership** — Build the support system that makes homeschooling sustainable.

I am not going to add extra methodology to make this look more complete.

This page is the founder story on my personal site. Homeschool Core is not this whole website, and this website is not an education business homepage.

**Privacy:** no children named, no ages, no photos, no family anecdotes beyond the fact of starting as a mom in Nigeria in 2023.
